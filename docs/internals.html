Budgeting & Unassigned

Unassigned is the money with no job yet. One function computes it, compute_unassigned(book, month) in apps/budget/unassigned.py, and every screen that shows it reads that function.

One rule behind every term: a claim is the larger of what was planned and what happened, floored at zero. Overspending is not a claim. It has already left net worth, so it lowers Unassigned the moment it is categorized, and the negative balance is carried forward rather than reset.

Guarantees

Planned money moves nothing when it is used

Income within budget, spending within an envelope, spending within a goal: net worth and the claim fall or rise together.

Overspending moves it once

It comes out when categorized. The negative balance is carried; budgeting into the hole moves nothing until past it.

Later months don't count yet

Budgets, income, allocations and transactions dated after the month count from the 1st of their own month.

What doesn't count

Void entries, uncategorized Inbox rows, archived goals. Hidden categories and hidden accounts do count.

One number, many screens

Pill, dashboard, budget and goals cards, quick-assign cap and reports all call compute_unassigned.

Still open

Illiquid assets (house, RRSP) count in net worth. The final name: UNASSIGNED_LABEL / OVER_ASSIGNED_LABEL are defined once.
